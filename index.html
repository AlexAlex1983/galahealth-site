Chronic respiratory diseases — asthma, COPD, and others — affect nearly half a billion people worldwide

Yet lung health remains consistently under-prioritized - in national strategies, in global financing, in public understanding

Global Alliance for Air, Lungs and Health (GALA Health) exists to change that.

Caregiver Inclusion and Support

We champion the systemic recognition of informal caregivers. By integrating their roles into health policies and social frameworks, we ensure those who carry the daily emotional and practical weight of CRDs are visible, valued, and supported.
