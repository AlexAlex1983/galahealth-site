Chronic respiratory diseases — asthma, COPD, and others — affect nearly half a billion people worldwide

Yet lung health remains consistently under-prioritized - in national strategies, in global financing, in public understanding

The Global Alliance for Lungs, Air and Health (GALA Health) exists to change that.

545 millionpeople live with a chronic respiratory disease
3.4 milliondeaths from COPD in 2023 — now the 3rd leading cause of death worldwide
~80%of people with COPD have never been diagnosed
~90%of COPD deaths under age 70 occur in low- and middle-income countries

Sources: GBD 2017, The Lancet Respiratory Medicine (2020); WHO COPD fact sheet; BOLD study, Chest (2015).
