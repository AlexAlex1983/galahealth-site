Bring the Lung Check to your community

Clinics, NGOs, patient groups and local health authorities can use GALA Health's free 2-minute Lung Check on their own website, in their newsletters, or with community health workers. It costs nothing and collects no health data.

1. Share your link

Use a link with your organisation's name, so we can tell you how many people joined the breath-test waitlist through you.

2. Add it to your website

Paste a few lines of code into any page. The check fits your page width and resizes itself. Answers stay on the visitor's device.

3. Run a pilot with us

Want it in your language, with your logo, or used by health workers in a county or district? Talk to us about a pilot.
