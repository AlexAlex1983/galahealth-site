Gala Health

Country Support

GALA Health supports governments to elevate chronic respiratory diseases (CRDs) from a neglected issue to a funded national priority.

Core focus

Our core focus is helping countries develop and implement national CRD investment cases - providing the economic evidence, policy analysis, and governance structures needed to secure sustainable financing and multisectoral action.

We work across the full investment case cycle:

Policy and Readiness Assessment

Policy and readiness assessments to identify gaps in strategy, financing, regulation, and data.

Economic Analysis

Economic analysis to quantify the cost of inaction and the return on priority interventions.

Multisectoral Coordination

Multisectoral coordination support to engage health, finance, environment, energy, labour, and planning sectors.

Financing

Regulatory and financing guidance to strengthen enabling policies and unlock domestic and external resources.

Action Plans

Costed action plans that translate evidence into implementable, budgeted national strategies.

Implementation Support

Follow-through support to embed CRDs into Universal Health Coverage packages, medium-term expenditure frameworks, and accountability systems.

Implementation Approach

Our approach is practical, country-led, and designed for delivery - not reports that sit on shelves. By strengthening policy, governance, and prioritization first, we create the foundation for sustainable scale-up of primary care services, essential medicines, prevention, workforce capacity, rehabilitation, and equitable access.

We help countries move from commitment to investment - and from investment to impact.
