Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how the Global Alliance for Lungs, Air and Health (“GALA Health”, “we”) handles personal data when you visit galahealth.org or contact us through it. We collect as little as possible and never sell your data.

Who is responsible

GALA Health, Rte de Meyrin 315, 1217 Meyrin, Geneva, Switzerland. Email: info@galahealth.org.

Visiting the website

This website uses no cookies and no advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from our own site. We may count page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses or build profiles of visitors.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, GitHub automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact form and mailing list

If you use our contact form, we collect your name, organisation and email address. If you join our mailing list, we collect your email address. We use this information only to reply to you or to send you GALA Health updates.

Submissions are stored in our Google Workspace account (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd) and forwarded to our team by email. Google may process this data outside Switzerland and the European Economic Area under appropriate safeguards.

Legal basis: your consent, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.
How long we keep it: contact requests for up to 24 months after our last exchange; mailing-list addresses until you unsubscribe.
Unsubscribing: use the link in any of our emails, or write to info@galahealth.org.

Lung Check and breath-test waitlist

Your answers to the Lung Check questions are processed only in your browser. They are never sent to GALA Health or stored by us.

To show air quality, your browser sends the town you type, or your approximate coordinates if you choose “Use my location”, directly to Open-Meteo. We do not receive or store your location.

If you join the breath-test waitlist, we collect your email address, the role you select, your country, whether you are interested in donating a breathing recording later, and your consent. We use this only to tell you about the Lung Check and the breath test. It is stored in our Google Workspace account as described above, kept until you unsubscribe or until 12 months after the breath test launches, whichever comes first.
No breathing recordings are collected now. If we invite you to donate one in future, we will first ask for your separate, explicit consent and explain how it will be used.

Voices stories

If you send us a story, we collect your name (or the name you choose), email address, country, role and the story itself. A story may include information about your health or the health of someone you care for; please share only what you are comfortable with. We use it only to read your story, contact you about it and, if you agree to the final wording, publish it. Nothing is published without your further approval, and your email address is never published.

Stories are stored in our Google Workspace account as described above and kept for up to 24 months, or until you ask us to delete them. Published stories stay online until you ask us to remove them.

GALA Brief

If you join our mailing list, we send you the GALA Brief once a month by email from our Google Workspace account. To stop, reply "unsubscribe" or write to info@galahealth.org.

Lung Check on partner websites

Partner organisations may show the Lung Check on their own websites. It works exactly as on galahealth.org: answers stay in your browser, and only waitlist details you choose to send reach GALA Health, together with the name of the partner site you came from. The partner's own website is covered by its own privacy policy.

Sharing buttons and external links

The share buttons (X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram) are plain links. Nothing is sent to those platforms unless you click them. Links to other websites (for example WHO, GOLD or GINA) are covered by those organisations’ own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email info@galahealth.org. You may also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
