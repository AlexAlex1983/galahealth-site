Focus area

Advocacy

Chronic respiratory diseases (CRDs) remain one of the most under-recognized major health challenges globally - despite their enormous human and economic cost. GALA Health works to change that.

We advocate to transform CRDs from a neglected condition into a clear priority within global and national health and development agendas.

Strategic Functions

Elevating the Narrative

We reposition lung health as a central public health and development priority — in national strategies, global health financing discussions and sustainable development frameworks.

Amplifying Champions and Best Practices

We identify, support, and elevate national and community champions and share best practices across countries.

Influencing Global and National Agendas

We engage ministries of health, finance, planning, environment, energy and labour, and city administrations — building the coalitions needed for sustained reform, anchored in hard economic evidence.

Our Goal

Our goal is simple but transformative: To ensure that lung health is no longer peripheral - but central to how countries design resilient, equitable, and future-ready health systems.

Because what is not prioritized is not funded. And what is not funded does not scale.
