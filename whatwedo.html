What We Do

Three pillars — country support, advocacy, and caregiver inclusion — that turn global commitments on lung health into national action.

We help countries build national investment cases for chronic respiratory diseases — the economic evidence, policy analysis and governance needed to secure sustainable financing.

Advocacy
