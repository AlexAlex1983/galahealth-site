Hear Your Lungs

How are your lungs today?

A free 2-minute check of your breathing, your risks and the air where you live. Anonymous, no sign-up needed.

Hundreds of millions of people live with a chronic lung disease such as COPD or asthma, and many don't know it yet. A lasting cough, wheezing or getting out of breath are easy to put down to age or fitness. Noticing them is the first step.

What's next

The breath test

We are developing an AI tool that listens to a short recording of your breathing, combines it with your answers and the air where you live, and tells you whether to see a doctor. Join the waitlist at the end of the check to be among the first to try it and, if you wish, to help train it with a recording.

Clinics, NGOs and local health authorities can run the check with their communities. Get in touch.

Questions

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It flags risk factors and symptoms that are worth discussing with a doctor. Only a health professional can diagnose a lung condition, usually with a spirometry breathing test.

What happens to my answers?

They stay in your browser and are never sent to us. If you join the waitlist, we store only your email, role, country and consent.

Where does the air data come from?

From Open-Meteo, based on the European Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service model. It is an estimate; local monitoring stations may read differently.

Who is GALA Health?

The Global Alliance for Lungs, Air and Health works to reduce the burden of chronic respiratory diseases, supporting health authorities to put policy into practice at county and community level. About us.
